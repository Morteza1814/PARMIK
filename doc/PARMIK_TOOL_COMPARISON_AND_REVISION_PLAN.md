# PARMIK tool comparison and manuscript revision plan

Prepared for Morteza, Jake, Ram, Ryan, and Professor Skadron. Literature and documentation checked on 4 October 2026.

This report compares tools for searching short nucleotide queries against large collections of individual unassembled reads. It connects their parameters and output capabilities to PARMIK's local alignment length and percentage identity criteria, and turns the supplied Bioinformatics and Bioinformatics Advances reviews into a revision plan.

The main recommendation is to retain the existing comparisons, audit their scoring and output interpretation, and pilot SigAlign, DREAM-Stellar, Lambda3, MMseqs2 nucleotide search, and Minimap2. A sequence index followed by a common alignment stage, especially Themisto or COBS, would directly address the reviewers' questions about candidate retrieval.

This is a literature and source-code review, not a new benchmark. Suitability assessments are recommendations; performance on PARMIK's precise workload remains to be measured. Exact installed versions and their help output must be captured before selecting experimental parameters.
